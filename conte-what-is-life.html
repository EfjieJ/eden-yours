What is life?

A text by François, in 46 pictures. Scroll or tap “Next”: the pictures follow each passage and appear gently. You can start the song and read while listening.

"What is life?" Life is an "eternal rhythm": Start, Continue, Finish, Start again. This rhythm repeats itself millions of times per second and creates the appearance of any form.

There is only one emotion, and that is "Admiration." All other emotions are just different levels of this admiration.

Everything is a "game segment." Each person plays their own game. The goal of the game is always the same: "to acquire data" - an understanding, a skill, an experience - in a limited time.

Emotions are simply used to give us the energy to stay in our game until we have acquired what we came for.

"Here are some concrete examples of games that people play:" A person plays the game of learning to play the guitar. She acquires the data "I am able to create music." A person plays the game of raising his children. It acquires the gift of unconditional love and patience. A person plays the game of building his business. It acquires the data of perseverance and value creation. A person plays the game of healing his body. She acquires the data of controlling her emotions and her health. A person plays the game of learning to really listen to others. It acquires the data of the true human connection.

Every tree, every object, every relationship around us is the result of a game that someone has played and continues to play.

That's what life is all about: everyone plays their own game to acquire the data they came for. And the more you play your game with awareness, admiration and without resistance to emotions, the more pleasant and light play becomes.

Eternity is not something we expect. It's already available to anyone who understands that he's playing.

Life is a "game," but not in the sense of entertainment. It's a game because it's based on "immutable universal laws" that never change, no matter the universe or the form of life.

These laws are simple and always present: - "Push" (start) - "Continue" - "Finish" (let go) - "Restart"

Everything that exists follows this eternal rhythm. For something to exist, you always have to push energy. Once pushed, it can't stay forever, it has to come down. That's the nature of the game.

The great trap is to remain fixed on the "structure" (the forms, the objects, the visible). True understanding comes when you move on to "function." When we stop paying attention to things themselves to pay attention to what they "really do," everything changes.

That's when we discover that we no longer need to snatch life to live.

When we really understand these universal laws - to push, to continue, to finish, to let go - and live according to them rather than resist them, we become able to live lighter and lighter, until we become "eternal on earth."

Language itself is changing. We're moving from the language of survival and structure to a language of function, rhythm and presence.

So life is learning to play in harmony with these immutable universal laws. The more we understand these laws, the more fluid, pleasant and free play becomes.

The End
