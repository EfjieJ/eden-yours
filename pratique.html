Pure Sensitivity

Practice

Simple doorways to shift out of the reactive mind, feel Function before Structure, and let regeneration settle in — including through continuous listening.

◎

Pure Sensitivity

Pure Sensitivity is clear attention on what is, without evaluation, without judgment, without invalidation. It opens the space where intention heals more than observing decline.

On Eden Yours, music is a support: letting tracks queue (Listen continuously. It regenerates.) is a concrete way to stay in Function rather than in the structure of “one more song.”

◇

The 30-second technique

A short invitation: for about thirty seconds, stop feeding the reactive mind. Rest attention on present sensation — breath, body, sound — without analyzing.

  1. Pause whatever you were doing for a moment.
  2. Feel rather than think: warmth, weight, vibration, silence.
  3. Let about 30 seconds pass without inner commentary.
  4. Return gently — optionally by starting continuous listening.

This is not a medical protocol. It is a poetic doorway into calm and presence, as offered in the spirit of Eden Yours.

✦

Conscious breathing

Conscious breathing helps shift into parasympathetic mode and calm the reactive mind. Inhale and exhale with attention, without forcing — simply accompanying the natural movement of the breath.

  1. Sitting or standing, relax the shoulders.
  2. Inhale slowly through the nose, feeling the air enter.
  3. Exhale gently, releasing the belly and the jaw.
  4. Repeat a few cycles, then — if you wish — start the library.

No promise of medical healing: only an accessible reminder to return to a calmer, more receptive state.

∞

The Body-Antenna

Your motility and conscious gestures can be lived as an antenna: each intentional movement becomes an agreement with joy, rather than an automatic reaction. Function (clear intention) precedes Structure (what the body “seems” to be).

Walk, move, listen — consciously. Let the music accompany this current of inner regeneration.
